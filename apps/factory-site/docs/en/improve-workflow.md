## Review evidence from runs

Open the workflow's **Insights** and look for failed runs, unusual wait times, or unexpected costs. Check Trace to determine whether the issue came from a node, the runtime, or the input.

## From Insights to a draft

Request a proposal based on the runs. Review the diff and rationale before applying it to the draft. Recheck ports, inputs, and approval gates, then run a test before publishing a new version.

> A proposal does not publish itself or change the version used by previous runs.
